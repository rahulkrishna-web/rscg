Research & Innovation

Where Ideas Become
Practical Solutions.

At R.S. Choyal Group, R&D combines abrasive science, engineering, automation and plant operations. We test ideas rigorously to understand what works and refine it into reliable milling solutions.

Established in 2012 at Arjunpura, Ajmer, our dedicated R&D Centre works closely with manufacturing teams. From material behaviour and machine controls to plant-scale performance, every idea is practically evaluated.

01

Abrasive & Material Testing

Studying wear, strength, surface behaviour and material performance for demanding grinding applications.

02

Controls & Automation

Testing PLC and HMI systems, machine logic, recipes, diagnostics and automated responses before deployment.

03

Flow & Process Engineering

Evaluating feeding, conveying, discharge and material movement to improve consistency across the milling process.

04

Plant-Scale Validation

Taking concepts beyond the laboratory and testing them under real operating conditions before they become part of a working solution.
