What is Choyal

A milling company that grew beyond the mill.

The journey began by challenging India’s dependence on imported abrasive products and flour-milling technology. Stones led to machines, machines to complete plants, and plants to everything around them such as silos, PEB structures, controls, testing, training and service.

What emerged was not a collection of separate capabilities, but one connected approach to making grain processing perform better.

01

Flour quality is a system outcome.

Consistent flour does not come from a single machine. It depends on how storage, cleaning, conditioning, milling, handling, controls and people work together.

02

Technology must work for the operator.

Engineering matters when it simplifies production, improves consistency and gives operators greater clarity and control over the mill.

03

Reliability begins after handover.

A system proves its value on the shop floor — across shifts, seasons, maintenance cycles and changing business requirements. Performance is built to last, not simply delivered.
