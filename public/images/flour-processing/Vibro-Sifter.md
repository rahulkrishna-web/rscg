# Vibro Sifter

Vibro Sifter is an efficient screening machine used for grading and separating flour, powders, and granular materials with high accuracy and consistent performance\.

### Key Features

- High\-precision screening and grading
- Compact and hygienic design
- Low noise and vibration operation
- Quick screen changing and easy maintenance
- Suitable for continuous industrial operation

### Applications

- Flour mills
- Food processing
- Spice &amp; powder screening
- Grain processing
- Chemical &amp; pharmaceutical industries
