# Grain Silo

A heavy\-duty mild\-steel silo designed for the safe storage and controlled handling of wheat and other grains in commercial milling plants\.

Built for dependable bulk storage, the silo supports smooth filling and discharge, efficient use of plant space, and integration with grain\-cleaning, conditioning, conveying, and automation systems\.

## Key Features

### Durable Mild\-Steel Construction

Heavy\-duty MS construction provides reliable performance, structural strength, and long service life in demanding milling environments\.

### Safe Bulk Grain Storage

Designed to hold grain securely while supporting organized inventory management and continuous plant operation\.

### Smooth Filling and Discharge

Optimized inlet and outlet arrangements support controlled grain flow, reduced material buildup, and consistent feeding to downstream equipment\.

### Intelligent Monitoring

Compatible with level sensors, load cells, temperature monitoring, alarms, and centralized plant\-control systems\.

### Modular and Customizable

Available in multiple capacities with customizable dimensions, support structures, discharge systems, and automation options\.

### Easy Plant Integration

Can be connected with conveyors, bucket elevators, cleaning lines, conditioning systems, and complete flour\-milling plants\.
