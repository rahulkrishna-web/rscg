# Atta Flour Silo\-

## Mild\-Steel \(&amp; Stainless\-Steel Flour Storage Silo

A high\-capacity storage silo designed for hygienic holding, controlled discharge, and reliable handling of finished atta flour in commercial milling plants\.

Available in both mild\-steel and stainless\-steel configurations, the silo can be customized to suit different plant capacities, material\-handling systems, hygiene requirements, and future expansion plans\.

## Key Features

### Reliable Flour Storage

Designed to safely store finished atta while supporting consistent quality and uninterrupted downstream operations\.

### Mild\-Steel &amp; Stainless\-Steel Options

Choose mild steel for durable and cost\-effective bulk storage or stainless steel for enhanced hygiene, corrosion resistance, and food\-contact applications\.

### Controlled Discharge

The discharge design supports smooth flour flow, reduced material buildup, and consistent feeding to packing or further processing systems\.

### Intelligent Monitoring

Compatible with level sensors, load cells, alarms, and plant\-monitoring systems for better stock visibility and reduced manual intervention\.

### Modular &amp; Customizable

Available in multiple capacities with configurable dimensions, discharge systems, support structures, and automation options\.

### Easy Plant Integration

Can be integrated with flour conveying, blending, packing, weighing, and centralized plant\-automation systems\.
