# Conditioning Silo

A purpose\-built silo designed for holding conditioned grain for a controlled resting period before milling\. It supports uniform moisture absorption across the grain kernel, helping improve grinding performance, flour quality, and process consistency\.

Available in mild\-steel and stainless\-steel configurations, the silo can be customized for different plant capacities, conditioning times, material\-handling systems, and hygiene requirements\.

## Key Features

### Uniform Moisture Distribution

Provides controlled holding time after water addition, allowing moisture to penetrate the grain more evenly before milling\.

### Improved Milling Performance

Proper conditioning helps soften the bran layer, improve separation, support smoother grinding, and reduce unnecessary process stress\.

### Controlled Material Flow

Designed for reliable filling, storage, and discharge to maintain continuous feeding to the next processing stage\.

### Intelligent Monitoring

Compatible with level sensors, load cells, temperature monitoring, alarms, and centralized plant\-automation systems\.

### Multiple Material Options

Available in mild steel for durable, cost\-effective storage and stainless steel for enhanced hygiene and corrosion resistance\.

### Custom\-Built for Each Plant

Capacity, dimensions, number of compartments, outlet configuration, support structure, and automation can be adapted to the plant layout and conditioning process\.
